The Story That Started It All

A boy from Ukraine was diagnosed with one of the rarest eye conditions. Thanks to a group of volunteers from different countries, he was able to undergo life-saving surgery abroad. Their efforts led to the creation of this support platform for others like him.

What is Brittle Cornea Syndrome?

Brittle Cornea Syndrome (BSC) is a rare genetic disorder characterized by extremely thin and fragile corneas, which may lead to spontaneous rupture, vision loss, and complications if untreated. Early diagnosis is critical. Learn more about symptoms, how it's diagnosed, and what you can do.

Diagnosis & Treatment Options

Accurate diagnosis often requires advanced imaging, genetic testing, and specialist consultations. Treatment may involve protective glasses, corneal crosslinking, or transplantation in severe cases. Our guide includes top clinics, surgeon contacts, and preparation checklists.

About Our Fund

Our foundation was created by a team of doctors, volunteers, and parents. We focus on organizing treatment, covering logistics, and raising awareness of BSC worldwide. Learn more about our mission, how we work, and how you can join us.

Full Story

It all began when a 9-year-old boy faced vision loss with no treatment options in his home country. A network of caring individuals from Europe, the US, and Ukraine stepped in. Doctors offered pro-bono consultations, a clinic in Switzerland accepted his case, and a fundraising campaign covered travel and surgery costs.

This collective effort highlighted the lack of resources and information for BSC families — and sparked the creation of this platform.

About Brittle Cornea Syndrome

BSC affects the eye’s cornea, making it thin, fragile, and susceptible to damage. If untreated, it can lead to severe vision loss. Diagnosis includes slit-lamp examination, OCT scans, and genetic testing for ZNF469 or PRDM5 mutations.
